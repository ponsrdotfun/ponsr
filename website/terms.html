What Ponsr is

Ponsr is a neutral tool. You mention it on X with a token name and symbol; it calls the pons launch factory and replies with the resulting contract address. It does not curate, review, endorse, or vouch for any token launched through it, or for the person who requested it.

Ponsr is an independent third-party tool. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by pons, by Robinhood, or by X.

What Ponsr does on your behalf

This is worth stating precisely, because it is not the usual arrangement.

  • Ponsr's own wallet sends the launch transaction and pays the network fee. You are not asked to sign anything, and you pay nothing.
  • Because Ponsr's wallet sends the transaction, Ponsr is the deployer of record on-chain, even though the token's creator fee routes to a wallet associated with your X account.
  • Ponsr does not take custody of your funds, and never holds tokens on your behalf.

Fees

Trading fees on a launched token are divided by contract, not by promise. The pons locker takes its protocol share first — 30% at the time of writing, set by pons and changeable by them. Of what remains, 95% goes to the creator and 5% to Ponsr's treasury. In practice that is 66.5% to the creator and 3.5% to Ponsr of total trading fees.

These shares arrive as the launched token and WETH, not as ETH. Anyone can verify the split on-chain.
