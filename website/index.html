Questions

Before you launch.

Ponsr is neutral infrastructure — a tool, not an advisor. Here's the plain version.

What is Ponsr?
Tag Ponsr on X with a token name and symbol, and it launches that token on Robinhood Chain through the pons factory, then replies with the live address. That's the whole job — it doesn't chat, trade, or give advice.
What does it cost me?
Nothing upfront. The Ponsr treasury fronts the network launch fee for every launch, so you don't need to hold ETH to start. Of the creator fees your token earns, you keep 95% and the treasury keeps 5%, through a fixed, immutable fee-split contract. Worth being precise about what "creator fees" means: the pons protocol takes its own 30% cut of trading fees first, and the 95/5 applies to the 70% that reaches you. Ponsr does not set that 30% and does not receive any of it.
Do you hold my keys?
No. A wallet is generated for your X handle by an embedded-wallet provider, and Ponsr never stores your raw private key. The wallet is yours, and it stays exportable.
Are these tokens an investment?
No. Ponsr is a neutral launching tool. Launching or holding a token is not an investment recommendation, and nothing here is a promise of returns. Most tokens launched through any permissionless tool lose their value — you launch and trade entirely at your own risk.
Where does the "what if" number come from?
From the on-chain buy and sell history of the wallet you connect — a record of what actually happened, not a prediction of what will. Illiquid or delisted tokens may show as unable to price.
