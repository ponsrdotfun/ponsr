How the record is kept

Three rules, and the third is the one that matters.

01

Only Ponsr's own launches

The feed reads one factory and one deployer. An older factory Ponsr used to call is excluded from everything public — it stays documented, not displayed.

02

Time comes from the block

A launch is dated by its confirmed block, never by your clock or by when this page happened to load. When the block time cannot be read, the page says so instead of guessing.

03

A failure never looks like calm

If the source is partial, stale, or down, the strip above says which — because an empty list and a broken reader look identical, and only one of them is good news.
